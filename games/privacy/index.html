Privacy Policy - AikiField Games /exercises/

Effective 2026. App by Richard Moon 6th Dan, student of Robert Nadeau since 1971.

The free practices require no login and collect no personal data. Progress (KeepDoingIt dots) is stored only in your browser's localStorage. A ?studio_id= deep link personalizes the banner for your studio; it is read from the URL and not sent anywhere. Optionally signing in with your AikiField account unlocks all practices — the page checks the same site session used elsewhere on aikifield.com and stores nothing extra. No data is sold.

Sign in with LinkedIn

If you choose "Continue with LinkedIn", LinkedIn sends us your name, email address, profile picture and a LinkedIn member identifier (sub) under the OpenID Connect scopes openid profile email. We use the verified email address to create or match your free AikiField account and the identifier to recognise you on your next sign-in. We receive no password, connections, messages or posts, and we do not post to LinkedIn. Visits that begin from a tagged LinkedIn link (?src=li) are counted so we can see whether the channel works; the tag is not tied to your profile. You can ask us to delete your account and these details at any time. Your use of LinkedIn is governed by LinkedIn's own privacy policy.
