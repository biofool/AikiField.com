What this is, and what it is not

Your answers stay in this browser and are never sent to AikiField. The cross-view is computed on this device from responses held in local storage. Nothing about it reaches us.

The buckets and interpretations are deliberately coarse. They are a prompt for a conversation, not a measurement.
