Tesshu’s Three Swords

The sword master Tesshu declared Three Swords,

three cardinal principles for leading change.

The Sword of Death / The Sword of Life

The Sword of No Sword

\*

The Sword of Death

The Sword of Death is the Sword of Discernment. . .entering boldly, it cuts, severing one into two. The Sword of Discernment is the sword of clarity, decision and certainty in action. Wielding it requires the deep presence of a unified spirit. The Sword of Discernment narrows the master’s attention to laser focus for the power of presence. Without the sword of discernment there would be no judgment, no self-reflection, no aspiration. Without the sword of death there would be no natural cycles of change, no endings. . .no beginnings.

The Sword of Life

The Sword of Life is the Sword of Love. . .it cuts so sharply that it cleaves things together. The Sword of Love is the sword of deep listening and compassionate understanding. Wielding it requires the harmony of principled engagement. The Sword of Life expands the master’s attention allowing him or her to join with others for co-creative power. Without the Sword of Love there would be no empathy, no joining, no family connection, no community. Without

the Sword of Life there would be no stewardship. . .

no social justice. . .no peace.

∞

The Sword of No Sword

The Sword of No Sword is the Sword of Not Knowing. . .it dissolves the Swords of Life and Death into the fire of open possibility. The Sword of Not Knowing is the sword of intuitive leaps, spontaneity, serendipity and synergy. Wielding it well requires trust in the natural flow, a letting go to inspiration and creative risk. The Sword of No Sword transcends power for self or others. Without the Sword of Not Knowing there would be no creative edge or innovation. . .no surprise

. . .no joy or laughter. Without the Sword of No Sword there would be

no inquiry, nor any doubt. . . no mystery. . .no discovery.
