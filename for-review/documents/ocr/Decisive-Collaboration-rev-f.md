***Distinctions in Language for Facilitating Dialogue***

Open and guide Dialogue through three phases:

I **- Induction / Presence** - Focusing Attention. Center& Energize.

Gather the group together in a circle. Acknowledge everyone as colleagues.

Define Dialogue for inquiry and opening vs Discussion for advocacy and closure.

Agree on a few facilitation guidelines: Be present. One speaker at a time. Select Dialogue Domain: Strategic / Reflective / Generative / Open. Posit a focus for inquiry, perhaps a key question or critical issue.

Rounds - Passing OK / Talking Stick / Popcorn / Open Floor

Facilitator maintains container, protocols and tools.

Check in / Check out

II -  **Integration / Harmony** - Sensing for Resonance. Blend & Join

Continually return into the sensory presence of each emerging moment.

Collaborate with open wonder. Discover key questions. Allow diverse points of view.

Listening over talking / Questions over answers / Speak to whole group / Avoid tennis

Reveal assumptions, exploring together where the known meets the unknown.

Listening to our listening / Inference Ladder / Seeing what we listen for

Mental models / Reactivity Triggers / Operating system of thought

Share what you see / I assume / I assert / I wonder / What if

Facilitator summarizes progress for navigation clarity

III – **Inquiry / Not Knowing** – Sensing for Spaciousness

Continually return into the sensory presence of each emerging moment. Listen for the whisperings, the intuitive unfolding meaning / Request silent reflection

Listen for the voice of the whole and the leadership of common sense

Surface collective intelligence / Listen for emergent consensus

Explore the border between the known and the unknown

Value not knowing / Sense for intuitive emergence
