Unified Field Experience

What if awareness, the light of consciousness, is itself our true nature, our essence ?

There are only two certainties. . .

There is something, not nothing. . .the play of experience / appearance. &

Awareness is real. . .all the rest is hearsay.

Reality is that which is certain and does not change.

Everything in awareness changes.

Awareness itself does not change.

So, awareness *i*s reality.

There’s only one reality.

So, there’s only one awareness.

Reality / Awareness is what is reading these words right now.

The name we give this consciousness is I.

What if the self and world views held by humanity were inside-out. What if we’re not consciousness contained in a body and generated by the brain looking out at a natural world separate from ourselves made primarily out of inert matter ?

What if all experience

arose and fell away in consciousness ?

were known only by consciousness ?

and were made only of consciousness ?

- Rupert Spira

What if we are primarily conscious awareness and only secondarily personae in form and name, rather than the other way around?

What if what we see when we look out into the world, is actually looking into the mirror of our own being ?

“That Art Thou.” – The Upanishads

*Don’t search for a unified field theory. It’s a unified field experience.*
