***Collaboration Explorations Guidance Documents***

>[*The Enterprise Covenant*](https://enquiryintoconsciousness.com/the-enterprise-covnant/) – The set of three critical agreements based on living systems leadership principles and Aikido requiring articulation, commitment and regular review for a senior team to achieve success.

> [*Finite and Infinite Games*](https://enquiryintoconsciousness.com/finite-and-infinite-games/)– A definition of the distinction between finite and infinite games and the characteristics of their players by J.P. Carse.

> [*Decisive Collaboration*](https://enquiryintoconsciousness.com/decisive-collaboration/) – An outline of the discourse and decision-making spectrums; a description of the distinction between dialogue for inquiry and discussion for closure; and outlines of distinctions in language for chartering leadership teams and facilitating their discourse and commitments.

> [*Facilitating Dialogue*](https://inquiryintoconsciousness.com/facilitating-dialogue-2/) – Four levels of facilitator focus for deepening dialogue.

> [*Leadership of the Spirit*](https://enquiryintoconsciousness.com/leadership-of-the-spirit/) - An early article with stories of how we used Aikido as a physical metaphor for dialogue and Bohm’s Dialogue as a verbal metaphor for Aikido.
