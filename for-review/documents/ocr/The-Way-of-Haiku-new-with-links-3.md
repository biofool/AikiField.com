*The Way of Haiku*

The Way of Haiku is a path of solitude, silence and deep presence in Nature. More than the Japanese syllabic structure by which it has been ill-defined in the West, Haiku is a spiritual path rooted in Zen, the Tang Chan poets, the Tao Te Ching and the Upanishads. Haiku means 'playful verse’ and in this tradition each poem reflects a direct experience of selfless awareness in Nature in which the poet disappears into the suchness of what’s arising in the moment.

[*Wandering in Wonder*](https://inquiryintoconsciousness.com/wondering-in-wonder/) is a collection of poems written over the last sixty years reflecting the beautiful, natural places in which I’ve been privileged to live and visit. They’ve been collected into booklets of seventeen individually written poems that were later arranged by season, weather and place.

[*Wandering Whidbey*](https://inquiryintoconsciousness.com/wandering-whidbey-2/)and [*Ravens Darkley*](https://quantumedge.org/ravens-darkly-with-images/) are booklets drawn from that collection.

The result is the lifetime of poems you’ll find in Wandering in Wonder. Critically, Jim also taught me za zen, awakening my life-long inquiry into consciousness.*
