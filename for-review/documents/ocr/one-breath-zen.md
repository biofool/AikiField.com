**One Breath Zen**

Notice the breath naturally arising.

At the crest of that wave, your exhale will release effortlessly. . .

Let your attention ride that deep sensory wave of breath

all the way down through the body

and rest in the open space

between breaths.

Abide a moment in that silent space between the breaths. . .

Notice the space between thoughts.

Notice the next breath naturally arising. . .

Simply witnessing this spaciousness between the breaths

is the direct experience of our true nature,

the light of pure awareness.

*“Let thine eye be single*

*and thy whole body shall be*

*full of light.”*

*Jesus of Nazareth*
