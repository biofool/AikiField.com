**Transformation Design Cycle -** Facilitation Notes

> **The Design Cycle** is a reflection of the principles and practices of Aikido. . .

*Presence* = Releasing & Opening the Body - - Dropping In & Sinking Open

*Harmony* = Releasing & Opening the Heart - - Connecting and Sensing

*Not Knowing* = Releasing & Opening the Mind - - Design & Declaration

> **The Core Tools** are expanded in a series of somatic exercises found on the Inquiry into Consciousness.com site in the Inquiry Lab Facilitator’s Manual.

*Deep Somatic, Energetic, Intuitive Presence* is key to the realization of all phases. Design facilitators themselves need to practice this Way of Being.

Weaving the *Three Breath Releases* into your daily activities is essential.

I – Inhale up the spine, release down – Attention > Body’s energy sensation

II - Inhale up, release down – Attention opens into energy field beyond body

III - Inhale up, release down – Attention opens beyond the known into source

*Crucible* – We have traditionally used this exercise before teaching the

Aikido somatics as a pre/post look at an individual’s initial reactive style

under pressure, and then to reveal learnings at session’s end – striking.

It also serves as a reality check for leadership style declarations at the end.

> **Phase I** – Emphasis here is on establishing energy awareness as presence and on using that experience to deepen group heart connectivity. The Circle of Requests is a safe feedback dialogue that surfaces potential issues & can be used well in Phases I or II. Leader goes 1st. Round 1: Strengths / Round 2: Requests/Coachings. Separate recorder. Respond only w/ thanks or clarity requests until end: Then agree / disagree / commit or wait for next meeting.

> **Phase II** – Sensing the relational field – concentric power circles / sponsors.

Basic situational analysis augmented with intuitive system sensing & visuals.

> **Phase IV** – Prime pump w/ intuitions. Consensual or negotiated decisions.

> **Phase V** – Intuitive Declaration - - Commit to the Impossible as Probable !

Prep > I declare that whatever I say is possible is possible !

> I declare that who I am is the stand I take ! [Step into your stand.]

> I declare that there is no fixed or right or wrong outcome !

> I declare [without evidence] to achieve the Breakthrough !
