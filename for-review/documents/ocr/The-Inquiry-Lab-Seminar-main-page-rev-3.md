The lab is a Holodeck for embodied exploration into the yin and yang of energy intensity, power and influence. In the lab we investigate Aikido as a physical metaphor for Dialogue and Dialogue as a verbal form of Aikido. With our primary postulate that Energy Follows Attention, we use slow motion, partner practices to validate that postulate through mutual direct experience and dialogue. In this way we establish and deepen participants’ core skills in personal and collaborative energy mastery for intuitive navigation and have a lot of fun along the way.

    > [KI] First we practice and test deep presence under mild pressure to ignite the personal energy field and, through play, gain familiarity and facility with its dynamics.

    > [AI] Then we explore extending our energy/attention beyond the limited mind/body to sense and play in, *and as,* the all-inclusive relational field of unitive awareness.

    > [DO] Those skills allow for the release of personal agency, even our ‘presencing’ and unifying, and our identity dissolves into open consciousness and intuitive clarity.

Once these basics are established, it’s about practice and application simulations in partner exercises or in the crucible. In essence we develop heightened sensitivity to energy through repeated direct experiences of Sinking Open into Presence under pressure.
