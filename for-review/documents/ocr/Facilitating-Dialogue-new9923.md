***Facilitating Dialogue***

***Dialogue / Discussion***

Open ... Generate ... Reflect .... Strategize .... Negotiate ... Debate ... Decide

*Purpose of Dialogue*

Deepen understanding / Seek common meaning / Collaborative learning

***Levels of Attention in Dialogue***

*Level I - Content Attention*

Clarity goal and type of dialogue - Strategic, Reflective, Generative, Open

Agree on lead question for focus / Raise assumptions into the light

Listen for underlying assumptions as well as presented meaning

Collect divergent views / Maintain spirit of inquiry

*Level II - Process Attention*

Hold each other as colleagues / Facilitator maintains container, protocols and tools

Maintain consciousness of location on the dialogue/discussion spectrum

Summarize progress for navigation clarity

*Protocols*

Examine assumptions / Explore the known and the unknown / Unfold complexity

Inquiry over advocacy / Listening over talking / Questions over answers

Speak to the whole group / Avoid tennis matches

*Tools*

Check in & out / Go around the group - anyone can pass / Talking stick/ball

Popcorn - anyone speaks when there's a natural opening / Request silent reflection

Ladder of inference / Left hand column / Assertions vs. assumptions

Begin speaking with 'I assert, I assume or I wonder or what if…'

Listening for feelings / Summarize views

*Level III - Inner Attention*

Maintain embodied presence - Breathing / Centering / Grounding / Returning

Listen to your listening - Mental models / Reactivity / Operating system

Value not knowing / Listen for your intuitive voice

*Level IV - Systemic Attention*

Listen for the voice of the whole / Listen for the arising, evolving meaning

Surface collective intelligence / Sense for emergent consensus
