**PRINCIPLES OF AIKIDO**

AS TAUGHT BY THE FOUNDER

* *The secret of Aikido is to harmonize ourselves with the movement of the universe and bring ourselves into accord with the universe itself.*

* *Aiki is not a technique to fight with or defeat the enemy. It is the way to reconcile the world and make human beings one family.*

* *Winning means winning over the mind of discord in yourself. It is to accomplish your bestowed mission.*

* *Aikido is non-resistance. As it is non-resistance, it is always victorious.*

* *My students think I don’t loose my center: that is not so. I simply recognize it sooner and return more quickly.*

* *A mind to serve for the peace of all human beings in the world is needed in Aikido, and not the mind of one who wishes to be strong or who practices only to fell an opponent.*

* *Aikido is the realization of love.*

You are here for no other purpose than to realize your inner divinity and manifest your innate enlightenment. Foster peace in your own life and then apply that to all that you encounter.*
