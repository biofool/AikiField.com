Wandering Whidbey
Christopher Thorsen 2020
Twenty snowy mountains and the blue green sea. . .
mirrored
in the gull’s eye.
Winging eagle high up in strong winds. . .
deeply piercing
endless blue sky.
Out on the bay
a raucous flock of white gulls
spirals down
on hidden fish.
Red tail hawk soars to stillness
in the wind. . .
every feather quivering.

In the forest,
three white rocks
stacked up in a pile. . .
Lady Susan’s stupa.
Hovering bee. . .mesmerized. . .
before the great unfolding
pink blossom.
Lighting on the leaf,
an insect folds up green wings
and blends out of sight.
Rain shimmering down through sunlight. . .
revealing this world
just as it is.
Marsh hawk drops a mouse to his mate. . .
passing below
upside-down !

Rabbit in scraggly weeds
|     | trying to disappear    |                      |     |
| --- | ---------------------- | -------------------- | --- |
|     |                        | in utter stillness.  |     |

Marsh hawk drops. . .
|     | and moments later rises. . .   |     |     |
| --- | ------------------------------ | --- | --- |
  talons filled
|     |  with emptiness.  |     |     |
| --- | ----------------- | --- | --- |

Spiraling in blue
  all round each other,
|     |    eagles in love. . .  |                    |     |
| --- | ----------------------- | ------------------ | --- |
|     |                         |   one cries out !  |     |

All those squalling afternoon gulls
|     |   standing silently now. . .  |                   |     |
| --- | ----------------------------- | ----------------- | --- |
|     |                               | the setting sun.  |     |

Reflected through the window,
|     | eagle circling              |     |     |
| --- | --------------------------- | --- | --- |
|     |   in the dog's water dish.  |     |     |

Hummingbird spots a likely flower
and zips
through the chicken wire !
Caterwauling coyotes . . .
only a few
completely fill
this hollow night.
Falling through moonlight
the white snow silences everything. . .
even the owl.
