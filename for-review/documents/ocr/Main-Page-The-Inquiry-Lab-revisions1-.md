***The Inquiry Lab***

The Inquiry Lab provides an experiential investigation into consciousness, power and the intuitive navigation of accelerating change. Its purpose is to enable Free Radicals – individuals who, somatically sensing the energetic field, move freely through complex human systems without resistance and lead innovative change.

The Inquiry Lab is comprised of five sets of guidance documents for practitioners who would like to carry this playful work further into the world.

>[*System Transformation*](https://inquiryintoconsciousness.com/system-transformation/)– a set of three documents outlining the critical criteria for effecting systems change, the necessary agreements to assure alignment of a senior change team, and the phases and tools required for the design and implementation of breakthrough results.

*>* [*Energy Explorations*](https://inquiryintoconsciousness.com/energy-explorations/)- a set of principles and practices for developing deep intuitive presence and personal Energy Mastery.

*>* [*Collaboration Explorations*](https://inquiryintoconsciousness.com/collaboration-explorations/)- a set of principles and practices for facilitating Decisive Collaboration in teams to achieve systemic influence and generate organizational transformation.

The Inquiry Lab Seminar has been successfully conducted with hundreds of leaders in over fifty corporations and non-profits world-wide.

*>* [*Inquiry Lab Seminar*](https://inquiryintoconsciousness.com/inquiry-lab-seminar/)– an overview of a one to several day seminar for groups of ten to a hundred people in which participants directly experience the principles, practices and applications of embodied Energy Mastery and Decisive Collaboration.

*>* [*Lab Facilitator’s Manual*](https://inquiryintoconsciousness.com/inquiry-lab-facilitators-manual/) – a step by step outline of The Inquiry Lab Seminar with directions for facilitating the seminar and the exercises that provide participants with direct experiences of the principles, practices and applications of embodied Energy Mastery and Decisive Collaboration.
