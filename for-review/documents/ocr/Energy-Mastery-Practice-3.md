Mastery Practice Design and Experimentation

I – Diagnose your energy mastery capabilities - Mastery Assessment
Form [Below]

         II – Declare your development vision and set some measurable
goals.

        III – Select some mastery practices that will serve your goals
and plan

	   a practice routine in which you experiment playfully with their use.

    IV – Enroll a practice partner[s] and/or enlist the aid of a
circle of supporters

	      who will help you stay on track and measure your effectiveness.

         V – Conduct your practice and personally review it on a
weekly basis.

	       Review your progress regularly with your partner or supporters.

	     Adjust your practice design and/or commitments based on results.

Guidelines for Practice

[Drawn from George Leonard’s book, Mastery]

Practice only exists in the present. . .practice diligently, but for the
sake of the practice.  Dedicate your effort without attachment to
outcomes. Love the plateaus that show no progress – they are a good
sign of progress. Practice requires vision and intentionality combined
with playfulness. Develop the fundamentals and take small incremental
steps. Play at the edge between competence and the unknown. Surrender
your perfection need. Acknowledge the negative, accentuate the positive,
and maintain physical fitness. Find good teachers, and remember,
homeostasis resists change – gentle perseverance pays.
