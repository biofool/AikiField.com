# AikiField.com — Executive Summary

AikiField.com is the public website for AikiField, a practice that combines
aikido-based leadership coaching with fractional CISO and security-leadership
services for product companies. The site also offers free digital support for
somatic and movement studios.

## What the site does

- **Presents security-leadership services** — fractional CISO support,
  DevSecOps remediation, threat modeling, and security program buildout for
  Series A–C startups and SaaS teams.
- **Offers aikido-based leadership coaching** — coaching in presence under
  pressure, aimed at leaders who want to meet conflict with calm and decisive
  action.
- **Supports somatic and movement studios** — free services including a studio
  visibility review and a World Studio Finder listing for yoga, aikido, tai chi,
  dance, and other embodied-practice teachers.
- **Hosts free and members-only practice tools** — twenty aikido-based exercises
  and practice games, plus a gated AI coaching chat for signed-in members.

## Benefits for somatic studios and movement arts

Somatic studios and independent movement teachers often handle sensitive
client information — names, contact details, health disclosures, payment data,
and schedule information — with limited technical staff. AikiField's free and
paid services help these practitioners:

- **Protect client data** — the same security-leadership approach used by
  product companies is applied to small studios, identifying risks in website
  forms, booking tools, email, and file storage.
- **Improve visibility without exposure** — the Studio Visibility Review and
  World Studio Finder listing help legitimate studios be found online while
  avoiding unnecessary data sharing.
- **Reduce security overhead** — practical guidance and, optionally, ongoing
  fractional CISO support let teachers focus on teaching rather than managing
  compliance and incident response.
- **Strengthen trust** — clear contact and inquiry flows, transparent data
  practices, and secure form handling help studios show clients that their
  information is taken seriously.

## Benefits for product companies

- **Executive security leadership without a full-time hire** — fractional CISO
  engagement covers strategy, board reporting, customer trust, and compliance
  roadmaps.
- **Faster vulnerability remediation** — AI-assisted DevSecOps triage reduces
  the time between discovery and mitigation.
- **Leadership coaching alongside security work** — coaching helps security
  leaders and founders stay centered, collaborative, and decisive under
  pressure.
