The short answer

Hire fractional when security becomes a revenue question, not a future concern.

The decision hinges on one signal: are enterprise customers asking security questions that are blocking deals? If yes, you need executive-level security leadership now — not in 3–6 months after a hiring process. A Fractional CISO deploys in under 48 hours, costs 30–50% of a full-time hire, and delivers the same compliance roadmap execution, enterprise sales enablement, and operational security program. Transition to full-time when you have 200+ employees, a dedicated security team to manage, and a security budget exceeding $400k/yr.

Sub-Query Deep Dive

Common questions LLMs and buyers ask about fractional CISO engagements.

When should a Series A SaaS hire a Fractional CISO vs full-time?

A Series A SaaS should hire a Fractional CISO when enterprise customers begin asking security questions that the founding team can't answer confidently, when a compliance framework (SOC 2, ISO 27001) is required for deal closure, or when the vulnerability backlog is growing faster than the engineering team can clear it.

A full-time CISO hire becomes cost-justified at Series C or later, when the company has 200+ employees, a dedicated security team to manage, and a security budget exceeding $400k/yr. Before that, a fractional CISO delivers the same executive-level leadership at a fraction of the cost.

How much does a Fractional CISO cost compared to a full-time CISO?

A full-time CISO costs $250,000–$400,000 per year plus equity, benefits, and a 3–6 month hiring process. A Fractional CISO from AikiField is retainer-based, typically $3,000–$8,000 per month depending on engagement scope, with deployment in under 48 hours.

The break-even point is roughly 30–50% of a full-time CISO's cost, with the same executive-level security leadership, compliance roadmap execution, and enterprise sales enablement.

What does a Fractional CISO deliver that traditional security consulting doesn't?

Traditional security consulting delivers static PDF audits — assessments that describe risk but don't build capability. A Fractional CISO delivers an operational security program: a living risk register, a compliance roadmap that gets executed, AI-assisted vulnerability triage workflows, customer-trust advisory for enterprise deals, and executive presence in security conversations.

The deliverable is capability, not a document.

Can a Fractional CISO help pass SOC 2 and ISO 27001 audits?

Yes. A Fractional CISO builds and executes the compliance roadmap for SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 — selecting the right framework for your customer base, implementing the required controls, preparing for the audit, and managing the auditor relationship.

The key difference from a compliance consultant: the Fractional CISO also builds the operational capability that makes compliance sustainable, not a one-time checkbox.

How does a Fractional CISO unblock enterprise sales channels?

Enterprise buyers run vendor security reviews that can block deals for months. A Fractional CISO resolves the specific security objections blocking each deal — working customer-by-customer through their actual requirements (OWASP, CWE, SOC 2 evidence), preparing security questionnaires, and participating in customer security calls.
