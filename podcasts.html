From Jug Band to Quantum Aikido: An Exploration of the Power of Harmony

Tai Chi Notebook

Host: Graham Barlow

"Aikido is the realization of love." — O Sensei

Richard Moon shares his remarkable journey from playing in a jug band and performing with bands like the Grateful Dead to discovering yoga and eventually Aikido, which he encountered by accident and learned as "a way of life more than a way of fighting." Moon Sensei describes developing a "very freestyle, jazz-oriented approach" to Aikido, which led to corporate coaching with Chris Thorsen and international peace building initiatives with Louise Diamond, including a $30 million project in Bosnia funded by Dan Whalen. The conversation explores centering, intentional transformation, and harmonizing with the universal movement through practices like the "universal breath" and the "drop back, sink open" technique.
