A good practice can still be hard to find.

Your work may be excellent, but potential clients may struggle to find it or understand it. The common gaps:

  • Your studio does not appear for relevant local or specialist searches.
  • Your website explains your method, but not the outcome clients want.
  • Visitors cannot quickly tell whether your work is right for them.
  • Booking, enquiry, and contact pathways are unclear.
  • Your Google Business Profile, directory listings, and social profiles are inconsistent.
  • AI search tools do not have enough reliable information to recommend you.
  • You spend time answering questions your website should answer.

The free offer

Studio Visibility and Digital Readiness Review

In a short review, we examine the public-facing digital experience of your studio. It can cover:

  • Search visibility for your location and practice type.
  • Website clarity and first-impression messaging.
  • Mobile usability and enquiry pathways.
  • Contact, booking, and class information.
  • Google Business Profile and directory consistency.
  • Whether search engines and AI systems can understand your offer.
  • Basic privacy, trust, and website-security signals.
  • Three practical improvements, ranked by likely impact.

What you receive: a concise Studio Visibility Snapshot with practical next steps. This is an assessment and a set of priorities — not a promise of rankings, leads, or enrolments.
