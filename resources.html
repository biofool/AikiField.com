Field Documentation

The following documents record the application of Creativity in the Unified Field principles in corporate and diplomatic contexts. Both are co-authored with Chris Thorsen of Quantum Edge, who has been Moon's student and principal partner for thirty years.

Leadership of the Spirit: Embodying Intuition Through Dialogue and Aikido

Thorsen & Moon · Quantum Edge

The Dialogue + Aikido methodology in three phases: Center, Blend, Lead. Draws on Bohm, Buber, Ueshiba, Einstein. Written from three decades of corporate practice.

Inspired Leadership Through Deep Mastery

Thorsen & Moon · Quantum Edge

Field documentation of Aikido applied to executive leadership. Includes extended testimony from James Dixon (SF Cellular One / Nextel EVP) on thirty years of practice. Named corporate case studies in telecommunications, chip manufacturing, and construction management.

The Inquiry Lab — Continuing Practice for Leaders

For readers who want to go beyond the book and investigate how to use the Power of Harmony in daily life.

The Inquiry Lab: inquiryintoconsciousness.com

Chris Thorsen · Inquiry into Consciousness

The Inquiry Lab is a structured curriculum of principles, practices, and exercises grounded in Aikido and the Dialogue tradition. It is designed for anyone (executive, educator, mediator, parent, or practitioner) who wants to develop mastery with intuition and navigate complex change with greater harmony.

The Lab is organized around three domains of inquiry:

  • System Transformation: generating coherent cultures, enterprise design, the design cycle
  • Collaboration Explorations — dialogue facilitation, leadership of the spirit, finite and infinite games
  • Energy Explorations — deep mastery, energy mastery principles and practice, inspired leadership

The site also includes a Facilitator's Manual and a historical interview with Thorsen on Aikido, embodiment, and deep dialogue.

The Inquiry Lab represents the living practice dimension of the work Richard Moon and Chris Thorsen have developed together over thirty years.

What to Avoid

  • Apps promising enlightenment in 10 days

  • Teachers claiming perfection
