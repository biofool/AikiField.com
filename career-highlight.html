Career highlight

Twenty years in security, nearly forty on the mat

Kenneth Kron has led security for growing companies for over twenty years and has trained in Aikido since 1987. Here is how the two fit together.

For more than twenty years I have worked on the part of security that decides whether a company can move: the review that closes a sale, the control a customer asks to see, and the picture a board needs of what could go wrong. I have led security services teams, worked on product security, and served as a CISO. The work is the same at every level. It means listening closely enough to find the few things that matter and then saying them plainly.

That approach has produced concrete results. At one company I cut the time to fix known vulnerabilities from 165 days to 21. I have led a team of analysts and penetration testers, analyzed the software stack of a phone, and built one-time-password infrastructure on HashiCorp Vault and Boundary. I was most recently CISO at Vigilent. Today I advise growing companies on security leadership and AI governance, based in Wellington and working with clients everywhere.

I found my Aikido teacher, Richard Moon, in 1987. Through the 1990s I taught children and adults at Aikido of Mountain View and Aikido of San Francisco, and later took over Richard's Thursday evening class at City Aikido in San Francisco. In September 2025 Richard awarded me a 6th degree black belt in Quantum Aikido. I have chosen not to register it with a federation. The practice is between me and my teacher, and it shows up in my daily work.

The two fit together in practice. A security review and a hard conversation both go better when you stay steady, notice what the other person is actually worried about, and respond before the situation hardens. That is what I train on the mat, and it is what I bring to a board meeting or an incident call. If that sounds like the help you need, I would be glad to talk. You can read more on the About Kenneth page.
