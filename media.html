about the embodiment podcast

The Embodiment Coaching Podcast, hosted by Mark Walsh, features conversations with teachers, practitioners, and researchers exploring the intersection of body, mind, and practice. With over 3 million downloads, it's one of the leading podcasts on somatic work and embodied approaches to personal development.

Mark Walsh is a trainer, author, and embodiment expert who has worked with organizations worldwide to bring body-based wisdom into leadership, communication, and conflict resolution.
